## What's New in v0.13.0

oh-my-pi (omp) joins the agents Flockbook reads: omp chats show their conversation in the Herdr app mod, Flockbook for Mac and the phone page.

### ✨ New

- Herdr app mod, Flockbook for Mac and the phone page: oh-my-pi (omp) chats show their conversation. Flockbook reads omp's logs in `~/.omp/agent/sessions/` (or `$PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR/sessions`) and knows omp by its program or its reported app name. omp's "/" commands, models and reply-box checks are not known yet. The Herdr app mod needs the Flockbook helper of this release.
