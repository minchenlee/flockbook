## What's New in v0.11.0

Flockbook for Mac is back: the first Mac build since v0.8.0. It brings the Mac app's chat in line with the Herdr app mod, and the phone page fixes to the Mac hub.

> The Herdr app mod has no changes since [v0.10.1](https://github.com/minchenlee/flockbook/releases/tag/v0.10.1);
> it is in this release too, under the new version number.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Flockbook for Mac: a Codex chat stays filled when Codex starts a subagent (for example its automatic approval reviewer). Codex writes a session file for each of its subagents in the same folder, and the app took it for a second conversation and showed none.
- Flockbook for Mac: a note another agent sends with `hc send` shows as one line, "↳ docs-worker: first line", that opens to the whole note on a click. Before, it did not show, or showed as your own message with its `<hc>` tag.
- Flockbook for Mac: Back from Settings opened over Search gives the search field its focus again. Sometimes nothing had the focus, and typed keys went nowhere.
- Phone page on the Mac hub: the message box sits at the bottom of the screen from the moment an agent opens. In a short conversation, or while one is loading, it stayed half-way up.
- Phone page on the Mac hub: going back to the list starts from where you were reading, with no grey square over the title.
- Phone page on the Mac hub: a note from another agent shows as one line with its sender, not as your own message.

### ⚙️ Changes

- Phone page on the Mac hub: opening an agent is a clear morph that runs to its end. Its row becomes the name at the top, a round back button comes in beside it, and the conversation rises in. The top bar is now two pieces: the back button (arrow only) and the agent's name.
